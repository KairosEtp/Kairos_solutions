Straight answers

A few useful things to know.

A good first conversation starts with clarity about what we build, where we work and how a project gets moving.

What does K-AI-ROS Solutions build?

K-AI-ROS Solutions designs and builds custom AI applications, workflow automation, AI search and assistants, machine-learning solutions, and prototypes for business use.

How does an AI software project start?

The team first learns how the current process works, identifies a useful and feasible AI use case, agrees what to prove, and then develops and tests the solution with the client.

Where is K-AI-ROS Solutions based?

K-AI-ROS Solutions is an independent AI and software studio based in Zlín, Czech Republic. The team works in Czech and English with clients internationally.
